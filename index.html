MAYHEM · Modular Audit and Yield for Humans Enforcement Mechanism

Six stages. Every claim traced to its source.

Each case runs the same structure - evidence, analysis, cross-pattern view, forward assessment, remedy demand, and how to use it - so a stage can be compared or reused across cases without re-deriving it each time. The fifth stage is a demand for specific, binding action, not a complaint asking an institution to investigate itself.
