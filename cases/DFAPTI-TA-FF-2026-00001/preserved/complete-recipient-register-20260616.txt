PURPOSE OF THIS DOCUMENT
The Enforcement Notice named three recipients. The Supplementary Tourism Submission named a fourth. This register consolidates those four and adds every other body identified as holding standing through the full evidentiary chain, including elected representatives, the newly created federal environmental regulator, and avenues available to any member of the public independent of government cooperation. Contact details below were verified against current sources at the time of compilation and should be re-checked before use if significant time has passed.

Recipient 6 — Senator the Hon. Murray Watt — Commonwealth Minister for the Environment and Water
Federal government — ministerial level (new)
Standing: Confirmed as current Commonwealth Minister for the Environment and Water, sworn in 13 May 2025. Holds ultimate ministerial authority over EPBC Act referral, assessment, and approval decisions and over the National Flying-Fox Monitoring Program. Under the EPBC framework, any member of the public who believes an action affecting a Matter of National Environmental Significance has not been properly referred may appeal directly to the Minister to request referral, or request that a state or territory government make the referral. This creates a direct public-to-minister pathway independent of council cooperation.
Contact: Office of the Minister for the Environment and Water, Parliament House, Canberra ACT 2600
Department contact: speciespolicy@dcceew.gov.au
Recommended use: Formal request that the Taylors Arm Camphor Laurel and colony management situation be assessed for EPBC referral status, citing the unaddressed Matter of National Environmental Significance and the absence of any referral to date

Recipient 7 — National Environmental Protection Agency — Chief Executive Officer John Bradley PSM
Federal government — new statutory body (new)
Standing: A new Commonwealth environmental protection agency has been established, with John Bradley PSM appointed as its inaugural Chief Executive Officer, as part of the Commonwealth's environmental law reform program following the Samuel Review of the EPBC Act. The precise compliance, monitoring, and enforcement functions being transferred to this new agency from the Department of Climate Change, Energy, the Environment and Water were not able to be fully confirmed in this compilation and require direct verification before submission. If compliance and assurance functions under the EPBC Act have transferred to this agency, it may hold direct standing over the unregistered camp and unreferred Matter of National Environmental Significance at Taylors Arm.
Contact: Status under verification — confirm current departmental structure and agency contact details directly with the Department of Climate Change, Energy, the Environment and Water before submission
Recommended use: Verify agency mandate first. If confirmed to hold compliance functions, this may be the most direct federal enforcement pathway available — potentially more direct than ministerial correspondence.
