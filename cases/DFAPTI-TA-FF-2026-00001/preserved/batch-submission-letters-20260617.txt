HOW TO USE THIS BATCH
Each letter below begins on its own page and is self-contained. Fill in your name, contact email, and date at the top of each letter you choose to send. You do not need to send all eleven — select the ones relevant to your situation, or send them all for maximum coverage. Each letter can be copied into an email body or sent as an attached page from this document.
Recommended order, if sending in sequence rather than all at once: Letters 1 to 4 (the primary recipients) first; Letters 5 to 7 (electorate offices and ministers) shortly after, referencing that the primary submission has been made; Letters 8 to 9 (watchdog bodies) only after the primary recipients have been given a fair chance to respond and have not done so adequately.

Subject: Formal complaint and request for response — unresolved flying-fox infestation, Taylors Arm Village
Dear General Manager,
I am writing regarding a flying-fox infestation affecting the village of Taylors Arm, in the Nambucca Valley Local Government Area, New South Wales. The matter was formally raised with Nambucca Valley Council by a local resident, Vicki Provost, at a public meeting on 16 March 2023, where it was recorded in the official council minutes that the colony had more than doubled in size that year, was roosting in Camphor Laurel trees at the rear of residential properties, and was causing tank drinking water contamination, soiled washing, vehicle damage, foul odour, and loss of usable outdoor space for residents, who rely entirely on rainwater tanks as the village has no reticulated water supply.
A review of all publicly available Nambucca Valley Council records from March 2023 to April 2026 — including outstanding actions registers, annual reports, and the State of Our Valley report — found no record of any council response, referral, or action taken in relation to this matter at any point across that period.
I am writing to formally request that council:
1. Acknowledge receipt of this letter and confirm whether any action was taken in response to the March 2023 presentation by Vicki Provost, and if so, what action.
2. Notify the Taylors Arm flying-fox camp to the National Flying-Fox Monitoring Program, by contacting Dr David Westcott (david.westcott@csiro.au) or Mr Adam McKeown (adam.mckeown@csiro.au) at CSIRO, or speciespolicy@environment.gov.au.
3. Commence a Review of Environmental Factors for the Camphor Laurel trees in which the colony is roosting, engaging a qualified ecologist, consistent with the process Kempsey Shire Council conducted for the same species in April 2024.
4. Apply for available flying-fox camp management grant funding once the camp is registered.
5. Develop a flying-fox camp management plan for the Taylors Arm site in consultation with the affected community, consistent with the Flying-Fox Camp Management Policy 2015 and the Flying-Fox Camp Management Code of Practice 2018.
I would appreciate a written response within 28 days. I am happy to provide any further information that would assist council in addressing this matter.

Yours sincerely,

3. Advise whether the situation at Taylors Arm — an established colony with no current management plan or registered status — raises any EPBC Act referral considerations that the Department believes should be addressed.
