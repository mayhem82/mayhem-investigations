Control conclusion
L-to-T confirms that the investigation's largest unresolved block is not whether PolAir-Remote can produce tactical results. It is the missing decision architecture linking those results to continuation/expansion: explicit thresholds, decommissioning rules, shared-governance authority, demographic exposure data, original Soteria cohort accounting, PolAir-linked bail outcomes and complete system economics. These remain primary-record acquisition targets rather than assumptions.
