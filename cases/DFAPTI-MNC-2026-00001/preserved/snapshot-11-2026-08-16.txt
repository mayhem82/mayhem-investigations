Scope: first acquisition pass against AE - Required Primary Records (Q694-Q733). This snapshot records what is publicly located, what is only inferentially indicated to exist, and what now requires formal information access. It does not convert absence from the public web into proof that a record does not exist.
Classification rule
PUBLICLY LOCATED = the record or an authoritative statement about it is publicly accessible. EXISTENCE INDICATED = authoritative material implies the record or governance artefact should or does exist, but the PolAir-specific record is not public. NOT LOCATED = targeted public searching did not locate the requested PolAir-specific record. UNKNOWN = existence/completion/content cannot presently be established.
F-201 - THE SIX-MONTH EVALUATION REMAINS A PRIMARY UNRESOLVED RECORD
Q694-Q697. Public sources repeatedly state that the Moree trial would be evaluated and that NSW Police would assess results before determining next steps. As at 16 August 2026, no complete formal evaluation, evaluation framework, original KPI document or public evaluation methodology has been located. Operational PolAir-Remote use continued into August. Classification: evaluation promised/expected - VERIFIED; completed evaluation - UNKNOWN; publicly released evaluation - NOT LOCATED.
F-202 - THE TRIAL BUSINESS CASE, RISK REGISTER AND ORIGINAL SUCCESS/FAILURE GATES ARE NOT PUBLICLY LOCATED
Q695, Q698, Q731-Q733. Targeted searches of NSW Police publications, policy listings, parliamentary material and procurement portals did not locate the original PolAir-Remote business case, trial risk register, stop/go instrument, expansion-threshold document or termination/decommissioning plan. Their absence from public repositories is not proof that they do not exist internally.
F-203 - INTERNAL RPAS POLICIES/PROCEDURES ARE AUTHORITATIVELY INDICATED, BUT THE CONTROLLING POLAIR-REMOTE RULESET IS NOT PUBLIC
Q705-Q709. NSW Police told Parliament in 2022 that commands requesting RPAS assistance must ensure planned use complies with law, the agency Operator's Certificate, and relevant NSW Police Force policies and procedures. The current public NSW Police policy index contains no document titled RPAS, remotely piloted aircraft, drone or Aviation Command operational policy. Classification: relevant internal policy/procedure layer - EXISTENCE INDICATED; controlling PolAir-Remote policy/SOP/launch/proactive-patrol rules - NOT LOCATED PUBLICLY.
F-204 - AI ASSURANCE HAS A MANDATORY NSW GOVERNANCE PATH IF POLAIR USES AI COMPONENTS
Q700 and Q243-Q251. NSW Government requires agencies using AI to apply the AI Assessment Framework across the lifecycle, record sign-off, re-run assessment when features/datasets/purposes/decision contexts change, and refer high/critical use cases to the AI Review Committee. NSW Police also has an AI and Biometrics Ethics Board, chaired by an Assistant Commissioner. No PolAir-Remote AIAF assessment, risk rating, AI register entry or board decision has been located. Whether the Moree operational configuration uses an AI component that triggers these requirements remains a factual question requiring the configuration/capability record.
F-205 - PRIVACY, CYBER, VENDOR-RISK AND DATA-FLOW RECORDS ARE NOW FORMAL ACQUISITION TARGETS
Q699-Q704. No PolAir-Remote Privacy Impact Assessment, cyber-security assessment, DJI/vendor risk assessment, data-flow diagram or PolAir-specific retention schedule has been located publicly. General NSW Police privacy, information-security, data-breach, records and governance policies are public, but they do not answer the Moree configuration questions.
F-206 - THE RPAS PANEL EOI HAS NO PUBLICLY POSTED OUTCOME
Q713-Q715 and Q284-Q306. Buy NSW confirms YR-2026-0058 was published 2 June 2026, closed 30 June and listed an estimated decision date of 20 July. Its page currently states that no notices have been posted. No panel award or subsequent RPAS contract has been located in the current NSW Police contract search. Classification: EOI and scope - VERIFIED; panel establishment/suppliers/contract award - UNKNOWN / NOT LOCATED.
F-207 - THE AVIATION COMMAND OPERATING MODEL REVIEW HAS NOT YET PRODUCED A PUBLICLY LOCATED RESULT
Q716 and Q307-Q312. Buy NSW confirms YR-2026-0012 was an exploratory EOI to identify aviation specialists for a possible future review and expressly did not procure the review at that stage. No public review report, recommendation set, adoption decision or subsequent review procurement has been located.
F-208 - COMPLETE MOREE TRIAL COST REMAINS UNDISCLOSED
Q719-Q721 and Q484-Q519. On 30 July 2026, in response to Brendan Moylan's request for expenditure incurred and projected full-trial cost, the Police Minister advised that the total cost had not yet been finalised and referred generally to NSW Police annual-report technology/assets budget information. Therefore the complete trial expenditure, recurrent projection and conventional-PolAir comparison methodology remain unresolved primary records.
F-209 - CONSULTATION EXISTS; THE CONSOLIDATED RESPONSE/ANALYSIS DOES NOT APPEAR PUBLIC
Q722-Q727. The official Have Your Say consultation confirms feedback was collected until 12 June 2026 to inform future training, policy and capability development. A Justice and Equity Centre submission is publicly available independently. No NSW Police publication has been located containing all submissions, a consultation analysis, a response-to-issues document, records of formal Moree Youth Crime Governance Group consultation, Aboriginal community-controlled organisation decision involvement, or a Closing the Gap impact analysis.
F-210 - THE 2026 NSW POLICE DISCLOSURE LOG CONTAINS NO POLAIR/RPAS RELEASE; FORMAL GIPA IS NOW THE CORRECT NEXT GATE
Q694-Q733. The NSW Police 2026 disclosure log currently lists four unrelated releases and no PolAir-Remote/RPAS/Moree-drone item. NSW Police states that records not otherwise available may be sought by formal GIPA application, with a standard $30 application fee and a 20-working-day statutory decision period subject to permitted extensions. IPC guidance warns that overly broad requests may be refused as an unreasonable and substantial diversion of resources and recommends narrow date ranges, known file references, exclusion of duplicates and third-party personal information. The acquisition plan should therefore use multiple tightly scoped applications rather than one 40-record request.
