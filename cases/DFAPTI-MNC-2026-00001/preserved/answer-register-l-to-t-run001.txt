RUN 001 CONTROL NOTE
The dominant unresolved dependency in L-T is not lack of a question set; it is lack of the PolAir-specific primary control records and linked mission/person/outcome data. The current evidence is sufficient to verify scalable capability planning, post-deployment consultation timing, proactive/covert use disclosure, Moree shared-governance architecture outside the drone program, Soteria/bail operational overlap, and substantial justice-system cost differentials. It is not sufficient to establish stop/go thresholds, decommissioning rules, PolAir demographic exposure, cohort attrition, complete bail outcomes, or net economic yield.
