
MAYHEM - POLAIR-REMOTE
PRIMARY RECORD ACQUISITION PACK
GIPA-ready segmented requests - v1.0 - 16 August 2026
Investigation: Moree -> Kempsey -> Mid North Coast PolAir-Remote
Question-bank basis: AE Q694-Q733, with linked questions throughout Q1-Q763.
Purpose: obtain the minimum primary records required to test evaluation validity, operational governance, privacy/AI/cyber controls, mission outcomes, procurement, shared governance and expansion decision architecture.
Filing strategy
These requests are intentionally separated by record class. IPC NSW guidance warns that an agency may refuse to deal with an application if it would cause an unreasonable and substantial diversion of resources. Narrow date ranges, known procurement IDs, exclusion of duplicates and exclusion/redaction of third-party personal information are used to reduce search and consultation burden.
Each request seeks electronic copies. Where a document contains exempt or operationally sensitive material, the request asks for partial access/redaction rather than whole-document refusal. Personal information of operational subjects, victims, witnesses and individual community submitters is expressly excluded unless already public.

A de-identified mission-level dataset for all Moree PolAir-Remote flights, with one row per mission/flight where practicable, including mission identifier, date/time, duration, mission classification, reactive/proactive status, originating incident/event category, general operating area or suitably de-identified geography, flight duration, result/outcome category and whether video was recorded.

Clarification: If a single mission-level dataset is not held, provide the existing reports/exports from which NSW Police measures PolAir-Remote mission totals and claimed outcomes, together with field definitions.

Suggested public-interest statement for each application
The requested information concerns evaluation and governance of an Australian-first police surveillance/aviation capability trialled in a regional community and publicly discussed as a model for expansion to other NSW communities. Release of non-exempt evaluation, governance, cost, consultation and de-identified outcome information would assist public understanding of whether government decision-making is evidence-based, whether claimed benefits are reproducible, and whether privacy, security, demographic and expenditure risks have been assessed. I do not seek personal information of victims, witnesses, operational subjects or individual community respondents, and I expressly accept redaction of genuinely security-sensitive operational material.
If NSW Police says the scope is too broad
Ask InfoLink to identify which record class or date range creates the resource burden before narrowing.
Preserve the evaluation/KPI/decision records first; they have the highest question-collapse value.
Reduce mission data to a machine-readable export of fields already used internally for reporting rather than requiring creation of new analysis.
Accept de-identified aggregation by week/month and broad area where individual privacy is a concern.
Exclude duplicates, attachments already captured elsewhere, and routine administrative scheduling emails.
If a document contains exempt portions, request partial release rather than withdrawing the entire record.
Lodgement facts
NSW Police states that formal GIPA applications can be lodged online through the NSW Police Community Portal or by post/hand to InfoLink. The standard application fee is $30. The statutory decision period is ordinarily 20 working days, subject to lawful extensions. IPC NSW notes that processing may be charged at $30 per hour, with reductions/waivers available in specified circumstances and agency discretion.
