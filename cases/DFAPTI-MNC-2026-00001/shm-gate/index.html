SHM Gate — Overview

Applies the operator's Structural Harm Mechanism (SHM) Catalogue - a set of named institutional-dysfunction patterns built from pattern detection across past cases - against this case's own frozen DFAPTA analysis. Sits after DFAPTA (Stage 2), before Lattice Atlas (Stage 3): a known-patterns check against evidence already gathered, distinct from Lattice Atlas's search for new, not-yet-named patterns. The catalogue played no role in what evidence was collected.

Interpretive layer, not a legal finding: the mechanism matches below are the operator's own structural interpretation applied to already-confirmed case evidence - offered for public scrutiny and further investigation, not asserted as a legal, regulatory, or judicial finding of wrongdoing by any institution or individual. The underlying evidence citations carry their own independent verification status; the mechanism labels are the analytical lens applied on top of them.

Scoped to the evidence freeze date: every finding here reflects the evidence register as it stood when this case's analysis was frozen (DEC-0009, 17 August 2026). Relevant developments may have occurred since - new statements, decisions, or publications - that have not been checked against this analysis and are not reflected in it.
