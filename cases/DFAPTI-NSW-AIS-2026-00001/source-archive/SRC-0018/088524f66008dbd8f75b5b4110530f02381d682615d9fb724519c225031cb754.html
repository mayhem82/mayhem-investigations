ICT Services Scheme

Update history

ICT Services Scheme purchasing framework’s contract templates have been updated
Updated: 22 Aug 2025

The ICT Purchasing Framework’s contract templates have been updated to include new artificial intelligence (AI) clauses and will take effect on 23 August 2025.

The contract templates for Core& and MICTA/ICTA contracting frameworks under the ICT Purchasing Framework have been updated to incorporate Artificial Intelligence (AI) clauses. These templates will apply to new procurements or when the procurement strategy is approved on or after 23 August 2025.

All NSW Government and eligible non-government buyers, and suppliers under the ICT Services Scheme must familiarise themselves with these updates to ensure compliance and harness full benefits.

Ensure that your internal commercial and legal teams are aware of the new updates to ICT Purchasing Framework.

If you have any questions about the ICT Purchasing Framework amendments, email the ICT Services team: ICTServices@customerservice.nsw.gov.au

ICT Services Scheme membership requirements factsheet released
Updated: 6 Sep 2024

New factsheet released summarising the key requirements for suppliers joining the ICT Services Scheme.

The ICT Services Scheme is a mandatory all-of-government arrangement that allows suppliers to provide ICT and digital products and services to NSW Government agencies.

This factsheet helps both new and existing suppliers understand the membership requirements.

The factsheet is available from two locations:

  • ICT Services Scheme page (how to supply tab)
  • Scheme module page

For feedback and questions relating to the ICT Services Scheme, contact ICTServices@customerservice.nsw.gov.au

ICT Consulting Commercial Framework refreshed
Updated: 28 Jun 2024

The ICT Consulting Commercial Framework has been updated and should be used from 1 July 2024 when procuring ICT Consulting services, Digital.NSW will hold sessions to explain the updates.

The ICT Consulting Commercial Framework PDF, 464.17 KB has been updated with new commercial and governance changes. All NSW Government and Eligible buyers should review these updates to ensure compliance and maximise benefits.

We are hosting virtual briefing sessions for NSW Government buyers.

The sessions will go through the updated ICT Consulting Commercial Framework and its governance arrangements.

NSW Government buyers can register to attend one of the following sessions:

  • Tuesday 2 July 2024 11:30am to 12:00pm
  • Thursday 4 July 2024 12:00pm to 12:30pm
  • Wednesday 10 July 2024 12:00pm to 12:30pm

View the ICT Services Scheme.

For any questions about the framework or briefing session, contact ICTServicesCM@customerservice.nsw.gov.au

ICT Services Scheme extended until 2029
Updated: 22 Feb 2024

The ICT Services Scheme has been extended for 5 years until 28 February 2029.

NSW Government buyers can continue using the ICT Services Scheme as usual. The ICT Services Scheme will remain open to new suppliers, and no action is required from existing suppliers under the scheme.

ICT Services Scheme Rules updated
Updated: 23 Aug 2023

Minor updates to scheme rules.

The Department of Customer Service has made the following updates to the ICT Services Scheme Rules:

  • updated instructions on how to apply for the scheme using buy.nsw
  • clarified insurance requirements
  • updated references to the ICT Purchasing Framework.
Changes to the ICT Services Scheme
Updated: 27 Nov 2020

The scheme rules have been updated for compliance with the Enforceable Procurement Provisions (EPP) Direction.

The Department of Customer Services has reviewed the ICT Services Scheme (SCM0020) and established a procurement list. The procurement list provides a procurement pathway that is compliant with PBD 2019-05 Enforceable Procurement Provisions (EPP Direction).

All buyers and suppliers using the scheme should familiarise themselves with the updated ICT Services Scheme Rules DOCX, 999Kb.

Supplier transition to the procurement list

All current suppliers on the scheme are deemed to be part of the procurement list. No action is required from suppliers to transition to the list.

Requirements to join the scheme remain the same. These are outlined in clause 9 of the scheme rules.

Complying with the EPP Direction when using the ICT Services Scheme

When procuring ICT-related goods and services over the $680,000 threshold, NSW Government agencies can comply with the EPP Direction by using a prequalified supplier on the ICT Services Scheme.

Limited tendering is allowed under specific circumstances. These are outlined in clause 14 of the scheme rules.

For more information, please email ICTServices@customerservice.nsw.gov.au
