How the gate works

Each candidate is examined in this order: 4, mechanism-grounded (a documented active mechanism in this case, external confirmation of its output class, and present operating conditions); 1, evidence-grounded (documented directional change, a strong founding basis, and direct continuation without an intervening variable); 2, conditional (a stated condition and evidence supporting a probable outcome); then 3, inference excluded where the candidate does not satisfy the required evidentiary conditions.

The decision concerns the particular assertion shown, not every possible future. Excluding a prediction does not prove its opposite. No invented numerical confidence or prediction date is assigned.

Limits and next evidence

The case can establish that a provider reported a restoration without establishing what happened to every affected person. It can record a dependency without proving that every incident involving it has one cause. Missing information and silence do not prove harm, remedy or negligence.

Evidence gathering and analysis can continue as these decisions are reviewed. The page flags when its underlying records change; that flag does not itself perform a new assessment or monitor systems automatically.
