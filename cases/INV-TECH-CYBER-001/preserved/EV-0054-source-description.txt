KnowBe4 reports that its product telemetry detected outage-themed phishing on 13 November at approximately 468 times its 9–11 November daily detection average; user reports were approximately 187 times that baseline. It describes four example lures, including held messages, a security check, an Exchange notice and a Teams-style message.

Limits: The ratios are KnowBe4's reported product-telemetry comparisons against a three-day baseline, not counts of attacks worldwide or evidence of successful compromises. Absolute counts and sample overlap with other vendors are not established by this record. The company separately relies on a preliminary Microsoft report for technical outage context.
