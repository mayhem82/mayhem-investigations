Source description: Australian Department of Agriculture, Fisheries and Forestry notice 235-2026, published 5 November 2026.

The department reported that a disruption affected lodgements submitted through two cargo lodgement services as of about 09:12 Australian Eastern Daylight Time on 4 November. It reported those services restored at 11:45 on 5 November. It said assessment outcomes may have been delayed and that staff used workarounds when lodgement reference issues were identified.

This description is not the original source. The source does not establish the number of affected consignments, individual recovery outcomes, exact onset, or a shared cause with later incidents.
