The trust reported it could not receive online musculoskeletal self-referrals and suspended that route from midday. At 17:00 it reported email and referral systems returning to normal and warned of slower responses while teams recovered.

The notice documents an actual suspension and a recovery update. It supplies no count of people affected, actual referral wait times, or evidence that the whole backlog cleared.
