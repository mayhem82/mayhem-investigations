Trellix researchers report observing 252 outage-themed emails across more than 30 organisations between approximately 17:04 and 20:38 Coordinated Universal Time on 13 November. The reported messages used service-health and held-message lures. Trellix describes a multi-stage route ending in a Microsoft-like sign-in page and discusses the risk of captured sessions.

Limits: The count is limited to Trellix observations, not a worldwide count. Trellix reports the same three sender domains and an intermediary destination described by Microsoft in EV-0021. That overlap supports a relationship between source reports, not proof of independent samples, a named operator, successful account compromise, or a cause of the outage. Trellix says its suggested link to a particular phishing kit is unconfirmed.
