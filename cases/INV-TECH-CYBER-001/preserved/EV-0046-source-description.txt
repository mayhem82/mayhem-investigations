At 10:30 the board reported affected digital communications, possible delays for NHS services including general practices, and a temporary telephone route for provider referrals. At 16:00 it reported uneven recovery and asked urgent senders without a response to confirm receipt by phone.

Limits: The notice is first-party evidence of what the care board published and its reported operational concern. It does not quantify delayed referrals, establish individual patient outcomes or independently determine technical cause.
