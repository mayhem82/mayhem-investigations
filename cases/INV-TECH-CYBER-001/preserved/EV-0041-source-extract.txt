The trust reported procedures were going ahead on 13 November, published telephone fallback guidance for time-sensitive referrals, and stated on 16 November that Microsoft services had been restored and its services were operating normally.

The statements are bounded to this trust and the stated dates. They do not measure missed referrals, response times, or outcomes at other trusts.
