The ambulance service reported an effect from the Microsoft outage, said emergency 999 service was unaffected, and said non-emergency patient transport telephone lines were busier than usual. It described using contingency plans for staff communication.

Limits: This is the operator’s account of its own services. It does not establish patient waiting times, missed journeys, independent technical cause or that all callers were unaffected.
