Willawarrin / Bellbrook Recurring Drinking Water Failure — Kempsey Shire, NSW

Six stages, each built on the one before it. Evidence comes first and never changes to fit a later conclusion; everything downstream - analysis, cross-pattern view, forward judgement, and any actual complaint - has to trace back to it.

Six stages

Stage 1 — Evidence

DFAPTI

The live evidence register: 71 primary-source items, chronology, sources, open questions, and the full investigation workspace. This is the record of what happened. It does not draw conclusions.

Stage 2 — Analysis

DFAPTA

Reads across the evidence register for patterns, contradictions, and open questions. Every path states what it does and does not conclude.

Gate — Known-patterns check

SHM Gate

Not one of the six stages - an optional check applied after DFAPTA, before Lattice Atlas. Runs the operator's Structural Harm Mechanism Catalogue against this case's own frozen analysis: 9 mechanisms applied with real citations, 1 flagged as risk only, 1 candidate new mechanism this case surfaced that isn't in the catalogue yet.

Gate — Yield assessment

Yield Gate

Not one of the six stages, not a checklist this case had to clear - Yield for Humans is always required, not gated. Assesses which of the Yield Catalogue's 11 Discovered Functions and 19 Citizen Tools this case's own material actually demonstrates. Unusually candid about its own limits: this case's own analysis states directly that "a direct community evidence stream is a baseline yield gap" (AR-025) - reported here as a gap, not reframed as a success.

Stage 3 — Cross-pattern view

Lattice Atlas

Looks across this case's own threads, contradictions, and open questions together, for patterns no single thread shows on its own.

Stage 4 — Forward assessment

Temporal Projection Integrity Engine

What the current trajectory suggests if nothing changes - stated as reasoned judgement with its basis and its limits, not as certainty.

Stage 5 — Formal complaint

Enforcement Notice

A submission-ready complaint addressed to the bodies with actual jurisdiction, asking for specific, answerable action. It has whatever weight the facts and the recipient's own obligations give it - nothing more.

Stage 6 — How to use this

Advocacy Package

Who to send the notice to, how to submit it, how to log what comes back, and how to independently verify any finding against its original source.

What this is not

No document here compels any institution to act. What moves a matter is a well-evidenced submission sent to a body with real jurisdiction, asking for a specific action, followed up and logged when it doesn't come. Every stage past DFAPTI is interpretation built on the evidence register - read it, and check the sources yourself.
