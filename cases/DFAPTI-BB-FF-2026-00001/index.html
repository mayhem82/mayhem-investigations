Bellbrook Flying-Fox Camp Management Failure — Kempsey Shire, NSW

A grey-headed flying-fox colony has occupied Bellbrook since November 2019. It's protected species - it cannot be forcibly relocated - but the same legal framework that protects it also creates a management pathway: a Flying-fox Camp Management Plan, which unlocks both formal management authority and access to a $1 million NSW Government grants fund. Kempsey Shire Council has one completed plan, for Rudder Park in East Kempsey. It has none for Bellbrook. A May 2025 community request for one was declined on budget grounds - though exactly what council formally resolved that day is itself disputed within this case's own source material (CTR-0001). Distinct from, but running alongside, DFAPTI-BB-2026-00001 (the Willawarrin/Bellbrook drinking-water case) - same council, same locality, a structurally different issue with its own document set.
