Active case · DFAPTI-BB-FF-2026-00001

Bellbrook Flying-Fox Camp Management — Kempsey Shire, NSW

A grey-headed flying-fox colony has occupied Bellbrook since November 2019. It's a protected species - it cannot be forcibly relocated - but the same legal framework that protects it also creates a management pathway: a Flying-fox Camp Management Plan, which unlocks both formal management authority and access to a $1 million NSW Government grants fund. Kempsey Shire Council has one completed plan, for Rudder Park in East Kempsey. It has none for Bellbrook. A May 2025 community request for one met a mixed response - exactly what council formally resolved that day is itself disputed within this case's own source material (CTR-0001). Distinct from, but running alongside, DFAPTI-BB-2026-00001 (the Willawarrin/Bellbrook drinking-water case) - same council, same locality, a structurally different issue with its own document set.

What this is not

No document here compels any institution to act. What moves a matter is a well-evidenced submission sent to a body with real jurisdiction, asking for a specific action, followed up and logged when it doesn't come. Read the evidence register and check the sources yourself.
