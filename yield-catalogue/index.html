Reference · Yield Catalogue

What an investigation can hand back before Enforcement is even reached.

The Structural Harm Mechanism Catalogue names how institutions produce harm. The Yield Catalogue - v1.0, MAYHEM-CAT-YIELD-001 - names the other side: what a community actually gets from an investigation at each stage, independent of whether a formal complaint or Enforcement Notice ever gets sent. Functions describe what the investigation is doing (translation, pattern detection, legibility). Citizen tools are the concrete, numbered deliverables that come out of it.

This is not the full catalogue Unlike the Structural Harm Mechanism Catalogue - supplied to this repository as a complete, canonical 68-entry document (catalogue/mechanisms.json) - the Yield Catalogue has never been supplied to this repository in full. Everything below comes from one source: the frozen Mechanism Register in DFAPTI-TA-FF-2026-00001, which applied the Yield Catalogue against that one case and described 8 Functions and 5 named Citizen Tools. The Citizen Tools are numbered 8, 9, 19, 20 and 26 - not 1 through 5 - which on its face means a larger catalogue exists that this repository has not been given. The Functions are numbered 1 through 8 with no gaps, which may mean that set is complete, but no standalone Yield Catalogue document has confirmed that. Nothing on this page should be treated as the definitive scope of the Yield Catalogue - only as what has actually been supplied and applied so far.

Functions (8 of 8 described, source: one case)

Every function below is the Mechanism Register's own account of whether that function is active, complete, or available-but-not-yet-produced for DFAPTI-TA-FF-2026-00001 specifically - not a general definition of the function independent of that case. Requires Verification, consistent with every other item carried over from this case's source material (see that case's DEC-0001).

Function 1 — EnforcementAvailable, not yet produced
The investigation had reached Lattice Atlas at the time this register was frozen. Temporal Projection Integrity Engine classification was named as the next stage before an Enforcement Notice could be produced.
Source: Mechanism Register, DFAPTI-TA-FF-2026-00001, 16 June 2026.
Function 2 — TranslationActive
The Lattice Atlas converted intersecting legislative instruments, grant programs, monitoring architecture, and council procedures into plain structural findings - described as removing the need for specialist knowledge to understand the governance architecture governing the situation.
Source: Mechanism Register, DFAPTI-TA-FF-2026-00001, 16 June 2026.
Function 3 — Pattern DetectionActive and complete
The corridor pattern - Taylors Arm and Bellbrook as a regional structural failure across two local government areas - was documented as visible and part of the record.
Source: Mechanism Register, DFAPTI-TA-FF-2026-00001, 16 June 2026.
Function 4 — Power RedistributionActive
Described as the investigation producing primary-source documented findings from publicly available records, without institutional backing, legal credentials, or council cooperation.
Source: Mechanism Register, DFAPTI-TA-FF-2026-00001, 16 June 2026.
Function 5 — Lived Experience ProtectionActive
The resident's account (EVID-006) was classified as primary evidence, recorded in verbatim form as reflected in council minutes, and described as unable to be reclassified as anecdote inside the investigation.
Source: Mechanism Register, DFAPTI-TA-FF-2026-00001, 16 June 2026.
Function 6 — LegibilityActive
Described as the Lattice Atlas making legible what council's obligations are, what pathways are available, what the grant programs are, and what the camp registration mechanism is - structural legibility the community was not otherwise given by council's non-response.
Source: Mechanism Register, DFAPTI-TA-FF-2026-00001, 16 June 2026.
Function 7 — ContainmentActive
Described as thirteen structural harm mechanisms having been named and documented as operative in the case, such that they can no longer operate invisibly within the investigation.
Source: Mechanism Register, DFAPTI-TA-FF-2026-00001, 16 June 2026.
Function 8 — Temporal InversionActive
Framed as the 37-month response void amplifying the documented case: every month that passed without response becomes evidence, not absence, and the Outstanding Actions register's silence across that period becomes part of the structural record. Also cited directly inside the Mechanism 10 (Time as Structural Harm Mechanism) entry in this case's SHM Gate.
Source: Mechanism Register, DFAPTI-TA-FF-2026-00001, 16 June 2026. Cross-referenced in SHM Gate.

Where this is applied

Only one case on this site cites the Yield Catalogue at all: DFAPTI-TA-FF-2026-00001 (Taylors Arm), in its preserved Mechanism Register. No other case's source material references it, and this repository has not independently verified any of the above against a standalone Yield Catalogue document - there isn't one to check against yet. If a future case supplies more of the catalogue, or the gaps above get filled in, this page should grow the same way the Structural Harm Mechanism Catalogue's own "Discovered Through Investigation" section does - by adding what gets confirmed, not by guessing at what the gaps might contain.

What this is not

This is not a claim that the Yield Catalogue only has 8 functions and 26 citizen tools - the numbering gaps above are strong evidence it has more. It is not a legal or regulatory instrument. Every function and tool above is presented exactly as this case's own frozen source material described it, carrying that case's own "Requires Verification" status - not independently confirmed by this repository against a canonical source, because no canonical source has been supplied.
