Reference · Yield Catalogue · MAYHEM-CAT-YIELD-001, current canon v3.0

What MAYHEM yields when applied — independent of whether Enforcement is reached.

The Structural Harm Mechanism Catalogue names how institutions produce harm. The Yield Catalogue names the other side: what an investigation actually produces for the governed population at every stage. Discovered Functions describe what the methodology is doing. Citizen Tools are the concrete, numbered deliverables that come out of it. Per the companion correction document (MAYHEM-CORRECTION-YIELD-001-v1.0, 29 March 2026), yield - not Enforcement - is the baseline the whole system is built to produce; Enforcement is one delivery pathway among several, not a mandatory terminal stage.

Discovered Functions (11, current canon v3.0)

Structural capacities that emerged through applying the methodology - not designed in advance, none ranked, none subordinate, count explicitly unbounded across versions.

1. Enforcement
The methodology produces findings with evidentiary discipline sufficient to route to bodies with standing. The Enforcement Notice names recipient bodies, identifies the legislative basis for their jurisdiction, addresses remedy demands to actors with the obligation to act, and classifies forward observations for urgency. Enforcement is one delivery pathway through which yield arrives. It is not the only pathway. It is not the mandatory terminal output. The chain serves yield. Yield does not serve the chain.
2. Translation
The methodology converts inaccessible governance documentation - subordinate legislative instruments, ministerial guidance notes, regulatory frameworks, calibration methodologies - into findings that are legible and invocable by people who are governed by those instruments but excluded from the language they are written in. The affected person does not need to read the instrument. They need the instrument translated into a structural finding that makes their account not just testimony but evidence that anchors a remedy.
3. Pattern Detection
Each actor in a governance failure sees their own piece. The minister sees the reserve figures. The regulator sees the conduct. The industry body sees the mechanism. The citizen sees the empty tank. MAYHEM sees all of it simultaneously and documents the convergence. No single actor can produce that convergence - it requires the full investigative chain. Once named, it cannot be unseen.
4. Power Redistribution
The full investigative chain can be run by a person with no AI access, no institutional backing, no legal credentials, and no platform dependency. Paper, methodology, discipline. The investigative power that currently lives inside regulators, law firms, parliamentary committees, and well-funded advocacy organisations becomes portable to the populations those institutions govern.
5. Lived Experience Protection
Institutional dismissal of lived experience is not a neutral act. It is a structural harm mechanism. MAYHEM names it, logs it, and converts it into a finding. Lived experience is classified as a primary evidence category from the moment it enters the record. When an institution reclassifies lived experience as anecdote, that reclassification becomes a finding in its own right.
6. Legibility
Governance instruments that determine whether someone gets fuel, whether their disability support continues, whether their water is safe - these are written for a professional audience that happens to exclude everyone governed by the instrument. The operational chain converts that material into structural findings written in plain declarative language, naming what the instrument does, what it was supposed to do, and where the gap between those two things produces harm.
7. Containment
When MAYHEM encounters a structural harm mechanism, it names the mechanism, documents its operation, and makes it visible. The mechanism can no longer operate invisibly after containment. Containment does not promise remedy. It promises visibility - and is itself subject to the same evidentiary discipline MAYHEM applies to everything it investigates.
8. Temporal InversionAdded v2.0
Time loads onto the institution, not the citizen: the longer a matter goes unresolved, the more that duration itself becomes documented evidence against the institution, rather than simply eroding the citizen's case. (Operator's own short-form summary of v3.0 - full extended description not yet supplied to this repository.)
9. Reflexive AuditAdded v3.0
MAYHEM's own primitives - the evidentiary discipline, the non-allegatory framing, the audit standard - applied to MAYHEM itself. (Operator's own short-form summary of v3.0 - full extended description not yet supplied to this repository.)
10. Impossibility DemonstrationAdded v3.0
Converts the closure of every available remediation surface into evidence of a structural impossibility of self-remediation - the absence of a working fix becomes a documented finding in its own right. (Operator's own short-form summary of v3.0 - full extended description not yet supplied to this repository.)
11. Divergence PreservationAdded v3.0
(Operator's own short-form summary of v3.0 - full description not yet supplied to this repository beyond the function's name.)

Citizen Tools Built

Twelve operational, paper-first tools in the current architecture - usable without AI access, institutional backing, or specialist credentials. Numbered as supplied in MAYHEM-CAT-YIELD-001-v1.0 - see the numbering note above.

8. The Evidence Register
A blank, paper-ready template for documenting evidence with classification, source identification, and immutable snapshot chain. Append-only - entries are never modified, only added. The register is the citizen's own record. It does not belong to the institution.
9. The Advocacy Package
The complete investigation output combined with Teacher curriculum documents - evidence register, analysis register, Lattice Atlas output, Temporal Projection Integrity Engine gate record, and Enforcement Notice, together with the recipient guide, canonical specification, and paper-ready templates. Designed to be worked through by someone who has never seen MAYHEM before.
10. Five-Step Primary Source Verification
A process executable without AI that teaches any person how to verify a MAYHEM finding against the primary source it was derived from - five steps from the finding back to the original document, data source, or public record.
11. Recipient Routing
The Enforcement Notice names the bodies with standing to act on each finding, the legislative basis for their jurisdiction, and the remedy demand addressed to each. The citizen learns which body has the statutory obligation to act, what instrument creates that obligation, and what form of submission that body accepts.
12. Lived Experience Documentation Protocol
Teaches affected people how to document their own experience as primary evidence - what qualifies, how to record an account in the person's own words, and how to produce a dated, signed record that meets evidentiary standards. Works on paper. The record is theirs.
13. Reclassification Recognition Training
Teaches affected people and operators to recognise institutional reclassification patterns when they occur, name them in real time, and log them as findings - converting the citizen from a passive recipient of institutional language into an active observer.
14. Institutional Response Log
Every institutional response to a submission is logged - date, form, verbatim content, classification. Append-only. Over time, a pattern of responses that acknowledge without acting, deflect without engaging, or reclassify without counter-evidence becomes its own structural finding. The citizen holds the log.
15. Harm Narration Construction
Teaches how to construct evidence-anchored harm narration for different audiences - regulatory bodies (anchored to the legislative instrument), journalism (built around one person's documented experience), affected communities (plain language connecting findings to lived experience). Structural findings document what systems produce; harm narration documents what those outcomes feel like and cost.
16. Onward Training Capability
Every module is designed to be passed on by anyone who has worked through it. The chain of capability does not require the Architect, a vendor instance, or AI access to continue - each person who completes a module can teach the next person from the module alone.
17. Operator Curriculum
Trains operators to run MAYHEM investigations across seven modules and four competency grades, from evidence-gathering-only up to the full chain with no AI at any stage - the continuity guarantee. The ceiling is determined by demonstrated competency, not access to technology.
18. Paper-Ready Templates
Evidence register template, path analysis template, Temporal Projection Integrity Engine classification checklist, and competency self-assessment - included in every advocacy package as the working instruments through which a citizen or operator applies the methodology with no digital dependency.
19. Non-Allegatory Framing
Every MAYHEM output describes system behaviour, structural gaps, and compliance architecture failures - never attributing intent, motivation, or individual culpability. This framing is itself a citizen tool: it means findings survive scrutiny that allegation-based framing does not, and the institution cannot redirect the conversation to the citizen's motives.

Citizen Tools Implied

Structurally implied by the architecture but not yet built or tested at operational depth - predictions, not deliverables.

20. Federation as Mutual Legibility
Multiple operators running independent investigations into the same system, preserving divergence, producing parallel evidence registers. The convergence across those registers - observable but not forced - produces a legibility no single investigation can. Each register stands independently; the pattern across registers is a structural observation emerging from the federation itself.
21. Temporal Accumulation as Citizen Power
The append-only evidence register and institutional response log gain weight over time. Absence is evidence. Delay is evidence. Time works for the citizen, not against them - the inverse of how institutional process normally operates.
22. Cross-Domain Pattern Detection
A person governed by multiple failing systems - health, housing, disability, child protection - currently experiences each failure separately. MAYHEM applied across domains for the same person or community could surface the structural convergence across systems, rather than treating each as an unconnected problem.
23. Community Evidence Aggregation
Multiple people in the same community documenting their experiences of the same system using the lived experience documentation protocol. Aggregated accounts - without statistical or institutional processing - produce pattern evidence the institution does not control.
24. Structural Literacy as Preventative Tool
A citizen who understands how a structural harm mechanism operates can recognise it before it completes - identifiable at the point an instrument is introduced, a consultation is announced, or an administrative instrument is published, before the harm is settled.
25. Parallel Verification as Democratic Infrastructure
Any completed MAYHEM investigation can be independently verified by any operator using the same methodology on the same evidence base. If findings hold under independent verification, they are strengthened; if not, the divergence is documented and preserved. Self-correcting through replication, not institutional authority.
26. Yield Without Enforcement
Chain completion is determined by yield delivery, not enforcement notice production. An investigation can complete at any stage where the governed population received what they needed - legibility, structural visibility, naming capacity, actionable understanding. The investigation runs as far as the benefit requires. No further.

The correction: yield is the baseline

MAYHEM-CORRECTION-YIELD-001-v1.0, 29 March 2026

MAYHEM's full name - Modular Audit and Yield for Humans Enforcement Mechanism - states the relationship explicitly. Modular Audit is the investigative methodology. Yield for Humans is the purpose: every operation exists to produce legibility, naming capacity, structural visibility, and actionable understanding for the governed population, independent of whether enforcement is reached. Enforcement Mechanism is one delivery pathway through which yield arrives - not the only one, and not a mandatory terminal output.

Chain completion is defined by whether the governed population received yield, not by whether every stage was traversed. Some investigations complete at Lattice Atlas, when structural relationships become visible. Some complete at DFAPTA, when the governance instrument becomes legible. Some require the full chain through Enforcement Notice. The chain runs as far as the benefit requires - no further, and no less.
