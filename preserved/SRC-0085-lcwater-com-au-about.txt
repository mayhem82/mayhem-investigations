LAURIE CURRAN WATER PTY LTD
LC Water is a process engineering house specialising in the bespoke design and construction of water and wastewater treatment plants.

Since 2001 we have brought reliability and confidence to treated water quality for water authorities and industry alike, be it for drinking water, industrial use, irrigation, or discharge to the environment.

Well known for our expertise in the Dissolved Air Flotation (DAF) and Filtration (DAFF) processes, our range of process expertise extends across a broad range of technologies including membrane filtration, water stability treatment, chemical dosing facilities, reverse osmosis, activated sludge systems, and all ancillary technologies supporting these processes.

We employ engineers across chemical, mechanical, and civil engineering disciplines and we carry out all design work across each discipline in-house, while electrical and structural design work is executed by companies with whom we have been affiliated for many years.

We deliver projects throughout Australia, Oceania, and Pakistan from our offices in Geelong and Karachi .

ABOUT
LC WATER was founded by Laurie Curran in 2001. Prior to founding, and since, directors Laurie Curran and Jarrod Fraim have built a wealth of experience in delivering water treatment infrastructure to communities all over Australia, and the subcontinent.

Similarly, our dedicated engineers, project managers and team members bring their own many and varied experiences in water treatment to our collective knowledge base, making LC Water a combined team of professionals for whom nothing cannot be solved.
