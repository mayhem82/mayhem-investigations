Terms, Acronyms, Codes and Identifiers. Kempsey Shire Council material runs on hundreds of acronyms, codes and reference numbers that are rarely explained where they appear — some meaning two different things in different documents. This page is the plain-language key, checked against Council's own primary sources, term by term.

Classification rules

CURRENT

Evidenced in current KSC material.

LEGACY

Appears in historical KSC material but has been superseded, renamed or replaced.

EXTERNAL

NSW Government, Commonwealth or industry terminology used by KSC.

UNRESOLVED

Code or abbreviation is demonstrably used, but its literal expansion has not yet been established from an authoritative source.

AMBIGUOUS

Meaning depends on context and requires disambiguation.
