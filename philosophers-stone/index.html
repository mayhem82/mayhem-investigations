The creation record

The colours emerged from failure.

The model is not painted onto MAYHEM after the fact. Its architecture repeatedly developed when a recurring failure was decomposed, understood and converted into a persistent governance mechanism.

Failure encountered→Decompose→Isolate defect→Explicit rule→Persistent mechanism→Audit / execute

Evidence integrity

Append-only evidence, source-before-analysis and provenance discipline answer overwrite, conflation and unsupported interpretation.

Continuity

Persistent registers and the resumption protocol move investigative memory out of fragile human or model recall and into governed state.

Correction

Recursive Audit turns a first fix into a test: check recurrence, check drift introduced by the fix, repeat until a clean pass is established.

Path of least resistance

Autonomy is not the objective.

It can emerge when repeated human corrections become explicit rules, persistent state and executable constraints. The relevant development is migration of routine function without migration of uncontrolled authority.

Human correction→Recognised recurrence→Explicit rule→Persistent state→Executable constraint→Less correction required
Propagation hypothesis

Transformation, not reproduction.

MAYHEM does not need to reproduce itself. A useful primitive can be demonstrated, trusted, adopted, altered and absorbed by another system. The name may disappear while the mechanism survives.

Demonstration→Utility→Trust→Contact→Adoption→Absorption→Primitive survival
Current position

The Stone is White.

Black processes continue when failures are decomposed. Yellow processes operate when governed evidence yields structural intelligence. Red-like processes occur when MAYHEM attempts or verifies external transformation. None of those, alone, changes the whole-Stone classification.

What White means

Substantial governed purification, persistence and increasingly executable discipline have been demonstrated.

What Red does not mean

More sophistication, more automation, broader reach or a successful intervention do not automatically make the Stone Red.

How Red is treated

Unclaimed. It must emerge from demonstrated behaviour and remain vulnerable to contrary evidence.

The unresolved question

What would the Stone have to demonstrably become before White ceased to be an adequate description?
