## 2. Analysis — Accepted evidence

### A01 — Aged-care oversight agency reports partial achievement of lived-experience measure

ACCEPTED — original statutory annual report, institutional self-report about its own performance. Reporting period 2025–26; transmittal 12 October 2026; publication listing updated 24 November 2026. Printed pp. 21–23 and 25 (PDF pages 24–26 and 28, zero-indexed); relevant text examined, including images of pp. 21 and 23.

The Office of the Inspector-General of Aged Care rated its lived-experience performance measure partly achieved. Advisory groups were engaged at least quarterly, but the stakeholder survey was changed from an intended annual cycle to every two years. The office cited cost, resources, consultation fatigue and time for implementation. Next survey: 2026–27. A separate stakeholder engagement measure was not applicable this year. Meanwhile the office exceeded its media, follower and speaking targets. It reported 0 new statutory reviews commenced and 1 completed (My Aged Care); these counts do not encompass every oversight activity.

Counter-evidence: the agency disclosed the measurement change, its rationale and partial achievement; continuing consultation and other oversight outputs were recorded. Unknown: representativeness, whether participants felt accurately represented in 2025–26, effectiveness of the changed cycle and actual subsequent outcomes. The absence of survey results is not evidence that participants were dissatisfied. The reported performance measures are not independent evidence of improved aged-care outcomes.

Inference: attendance at consultations, accurate representation of lived experience, and verification by affected people are different conditions. Public influence metrics cannot substitute for the latter.

### A02 — Paid financial-reporting infringement notices do not establish convictions

ACCEPTED RETROSPECTIVELY — ASIC release 26-188MR, 10 August 2026, date corroborated by Reporting and audit update Issue 5. The official notices register lists the three notices dated 31 July 2026.

ASIC reports three Mainfreight Group companies each paid $198,000 (total $594,000) in notices for alleged late lodgement of financial reports for the year ending 31 March 2025, due 31 July 2025. ASIC expressly distinguishes payment from an admission of liability or criminal conviction. Its contemporaneous announcement reports 27 notices exceeding $5 million issued since the broader surveillance began in August 2025; subsequent aggregate evidence already admitted in SR-508 (26-268MR) must not be added to this historical subtotal.

Counter-evidence: paid notices establish the reported enforcement response; the regulator explicitly recommends scheduling audit and approval processes to meet reporting deadlines. Unknown: individual notice contents, actual date of outstanding lodgement, company responses beyond payment, subsequent compliance and stakeholder consequences.

Inference: timely financial disclosure, payment of an infringement notice, eventual document availability and sustained compliance are separate conditions. The specific historical event is admitted as supplementary evidence, not a new November incident. No prior explicit Mainfreight admission was located in the targeted screening; historical duplication remains possible.

## 3. Findings — Longitudinal analysis

M01 — The oversight body's own lived-experience measurement is particularly relevant to SR-523 and SR-528: a person may participate in consultation without a current measurement of whether their account was accurately reflected. The disclosed survey change illustrates a genuine tradeoff between evidence frequency and participant burden. Neither a favourable nor an adverse actual lived-experience outcome can be inferred from the missing measurement alone. The institution's transparent reporting is positive accountability evidence.

M02 — Time-dependent transparency (SR-523, SR-524): financial statements are useful to stakeholders at particular decision points. Enforcement and subsequent document recovery are not interchangeable. A02 is a historical case, not additional aggregate penalty money beyond previously admitted totals.

C01 — Evidence-gap resolution, SR-528 H06: original IGAC annual report retrieved and relevant performance sections examined. This updates retrieval status, not a correction of an erroneous earlier substantive finding. Prior report unchanged. No other substantive correction established.

## 4. Conclusion

Participation does not independently establish accurate representation. Reduced measurement frequency does not independently establish reduced engagement. Enforcement payments do not establish convictions or complete restoration of transparency. No coordinated misconduct, intentional suppression, current Bellbrook-specific service failure or causal human harm established by these items.
