## 2. Analysis — Accepted

### A01 — Scheduled election date differs from actual declaration
ACCEPTED RETROSPECTIVELY — original NSW Electoral Commission outcome page and earlier election notice. Scheduled date 28 November 2026; declaration 29 October 2026; earlier nomination notice updated 19 October 2026.
The Commission records that the single nominee, Barry Knight, was declared elected to Cobar Shire Council on 29 October because the election was uncontested. The earlier notice described attendance and postal voting conditionally on a contested election. The general elections index still displays 28 November but identifies the election as uncontested. Earlier notices therefore cannot establish that polling occurred today. Counter-evidence: the current official outcome clearly discloses the status, and the earlier notice expressly anticipated the conditional nature of voting. Unknown: reasons no other candidates nominated, actual public awareness and subsequent council performance. No electoral irregularity or inadequate representation is established. No equivalent outcome for Kempsey is inferred.

### A02 — Free public recreation remains dependent on transport access
ACCEPTED INCREMENTAL — NSW Government operational visitor information for the season beginning 27 November 2026, observed 28 November; exact page update date unknown. Extends SR-518's reopening evidence.
The government specifies car-only access to Penrith Beach, limited first-come parking, free parking including accessible spaces, accessible toilets and lifeguard supervision during operating hours. These are distinct from the advertised free admission in SR-518. Counter-evidence: accessible facilities, free parking, longer season and reported safety measures provide genuine benefits. Unknown: actual transport arrangements for people without cars, possible community transport, parking demand, visitor demographics, water quality results and individual exclusion. No unlawful discrimination or actual denied entry established.
