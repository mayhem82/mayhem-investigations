## 3. Findings

M01 (inference) — Evidence integrity requires maintaining original testimony, original external analysis, subsequent edits, the justification for those edits and final publication as separate objects. Delay and aggregation can impair evidence before editorial review begins. Agency scope boundaries may be legitimate without resolving cross-agency human outcomes. This extends SR-543's lived-experience and recovery accountability work, and SR-538's assurance boundaries. No deliberate suppression established.

M02 (inference) — A centre opening is not necessarily full service readiness, and reported assistance is not proof of resolved need. This extends SR-536's distinction between infrastructure and operational capacity.

M03 — Sequential election snapshots must remain intact; processing progress is not an electoral declaration. Extends SR-541 and SR-543.

C01 — Append-only continuity: SR-543 H07 is partly resolved by recovery of the 70-page agency-comment report; original external report and underlying testimony remain outstanding. SR-543's classification discrepancy is not resolved here.
C02 — Append-only continuity: SR-543 A04 remains a valid earlier count; A02 is a later snapshot, not a correction to it.

## 4. Conclusion

The strongest incremental evidence is the authority's documented transformation of externally prepared disaster-review evidence and its recognition of limitations arising from combined events, delay and staff turnover. Legitimate correction and evidence erosion must be distinguished through version comparison. No proven deliberate misconduct, causal injury, new Bellbrook-specific failure or final Victorian electoral result is established.
