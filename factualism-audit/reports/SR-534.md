## 2. Analysis — Accepted evidence
### A01 — Postal voting relies on operational delivery beyond the electoral authority
ACCEPTED — original Victorian Electoral Commission release, 20 November 2026, concerning the election of 28 November. This is incremental operational evidence rather than a new admission of the previously examined voting accessibility arrangements (SR-518).
FACT (institutional statement): postal applications closed at 6 pm 25 November; the release estimates 750,000 postal voters, not a verified turnout. Completed ballots may be mailed by 6 pm on election day or handed in at a voting centre before closing. Australia Post reports additional staff, weekend deliveries, priority sorting and dedicated collection teams. Application forms were available at post offices.
Counter-evidence: physical drop-off is an alternative to postal transmission, and the logistics provider describes concrete capacity measures.
UNKNOWN: actual postal participation, late or rejected ballots, regional delivery performance, whether capacity measures were sufficient, and election results. No independently verified delivery dataset examined.
INFERENCE: electoral access depends on successful delivery and return of voting materials as well as the legal right to vote. The handoff to an external logistics provider creates a separate point at which performance must be verified. Neither the announced measures nor their existence establishes that any voter was excluded.
Source: https://www.vec.vic.gov.au/about-us/media/media-releases/last-chance-to-apply-for-a-postal-vote-for-the-state-election
No explicit previous admission of this specific logistics evidence was identified in targeted screening; historical duplication remains possible.

### A02 — Interim regulatory leadership and permanent appointment are distinct
ACCEPTED — original NSW ministerial announcement, 24 November 2026.
FACT (institutional statement): the NSW Independent Casino Commission announced leadership arrangements to fill upcoming vacancies. An existing commissioner was appointed Deputy Chief Commissioner for six months and will act as Chief Commissioner during the permanent recruitment process. A separate commissioner was appointed for four years. The government reports merit-based selection and relevant regulatory and governance experience. The Commission's functions include casino compliance, gambling-harm minimisation and integrity oversight.
Counter-evidence: an interim leadership pathway and a longer-term commissioner appointment are explicitly identified; the temporary appointment is not evidence of a leadership vacuum.
UNKNOWN: exact commencement dates, permanent appointment outcome, operational independence and decision-making continuity, workload, enforcement outcomes and measured gambling harm. Selection records and appointment instruments not independently examined.
INFERENCE: temporary continuity arrangements, permanent governance stability and effective regulatory protection are separate evidentiary conditions. The announcement establishes stated arrangements, not improved or diminished regulatory performance.
Source: https://www.nsw.gov.au/departments-and-agencies/dciths/ministerial-media-releases/appointments-to-nicc
No explicit earlier admission located; historical novelty cannot be guaranteed.

## 3. Findings
M01 — Electoral delivery dependencies. SR-518 established channel-specific access; SR-523 established local electoral access arrangements. SR-534 identifies concrete logistical capacity measures for a separate electoral channel. Legal availability, receipt of materials, accessible completion, timely return and acceptance into the count must each be distinguished. This is a descriptive sequence, not predictive modelling. The alternative drop-off pathway is positive evidence, not assurance that everyone can reach it.
M02 — Continuity through leadership change. SR-521 distinguished judicial appointments from net judicial capacity; SR-526 examined accountability fragmentation during governance changes. The NICC announcement supplies constructive evidence of a defined interim arrangement while permanent recruitment proceeds. It is not evidence of the same deficiencies in the casino regulator.
M03 — Duplicate discrimination. Direct retrieval confirms the Northern Rivers housing transition was already admitted under SR-521 A03. Historical references improve screening but are not substitutes for missing originals.
No correction to an earlier substantive finding established.

## 4. Conclusion
The strongest incremental evidence is the VEC/Australia Post operational arrangement for postal voting. An available right is not necessarily a completed access pathway. The casino regulator's appointments demonstrate stated continuity arrangements, not independently established regulatory effectiveness. No election result, actual electoral exclusion, coordinated misconduct, regulatory capture or new Bellbrook-specific failure established.
