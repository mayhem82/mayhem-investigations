## 2. Analysis — accepted evidence

### A01 — Apparent failure to vote is not an established infringement
ACCEPTED — retrospective/current operational primary-source guidance; observed 28 November 2026. VEC guidance publication date not displayed. Applicable fine period 1 July 2026–30 June 2027. 
FACT: VEC describes a preliminary notice allowing 28 days to explain apparent non-voting or identify where/how a vote was cast. An infringement notice follows if the response is absent, late or rejected; the relevant fine is $105. Review, court determination and hardship payment arrangements are available at specified stages. An internal reviewer is not the original decision-maker; only one internal review per infringement is available. Once referred to Fines Victoria, VEC no longer considers payment plans, extensions or internal reviews.
Counter-evidence: the initial opportunity to correct records, review and court access are explicit safeguards. Fines Victoria separately describes downstream options for 2024 council-election fines in its 19 January 2026 notice; this is not evidence of enforcement arising from today's election.
UNKNOWN: actual 2026 non-voting notices, weather-related exemptions, review acceptance, delivery failures, accessibility of review, actual fines and individual outcomes. No automatic exemption or individual eligibility determined.
INFERENCE: A system's apparent non-participation signal needs a correction pathway before sanction; practical accessibility of that pathway is separate from its existence. The person may need to respond to preserve their rights. Extend SR-534, SR-536, SR-541 without asserting that storm closures caused exclusion.

### A02 — ASIC footnote clarifies announced remediation versus payments completed
ACCEPTED — incremental primary-source clarification of previously held candidate SR-541 H07 (and earlier holds). ASIC release 26-162MR; reporting period July 2025–June 2026; publication day not verified.
FACT: ASIC reports $830 million in court-ordered civil penalties for 2025–26, including $480 million ordered in January–June 2026. Its reported remediation total is $643.5 million (rounded to $644 million in headline). The background note explicitly defines that total by announcements made during the reporting period, not payments necessarily made in that period. It says payments can occur outside the period and programme totals can change.
Counter-evidence: ASIC gives examples of actual compensation and refunds, and discloses the aggregate's scope. This is a constructive transparency safeguard, not proof of misrepresentation.
UNKNOWN: consolidated amount actually paid, outstanding balance, case-level reconciliation, duplicate amounts across reports, and ultimate recipient outcomes. No additional amount counted as recovered money or new individual enforcement incident.
INFERENCE: announcement-period remediation is a separate metric from cash-period restitution, just as penalty orders are separate from payment. Retain the source footnote with any subsequent use of the headline.

## 3. Findings

M01 — Detection, correction, enforcement and review are distinct stages. The VEC's preliminary notice is a concrete counterexample to an assumption that apparent non-participation immediately establishes liability. Actual notice delivery, response accessibility and outcomes remain unverified.
M02 — Financial remediation reporting requires a measurement boundary. The regulator's own footnote resolves what its aggregate measures, not the final cash actually paid. Do not add this aggregate to previously counted case-level remediation.
M03 — Historical duplicate discrimination must include unavailable originals. The NSW supported decision-making and forensic tribunal records are substantial but probable historical duplicates; recovering the underlying institutional source does not recover the original MAYHEM report. The former's findings on decision support and latter's proposed communications safeguards remain relevant to human agency, without counting either again.
C01 — Append-only clarification to SR-541 H07: ASIC's definition of the headline remediation figure has been recovered; full paid/outstanding reconciliation remains unknown. Prior report unchanged.

## 4. Conclusion

The strongest incremental finding is the explicit distinction between announced remediation and realised payment. Electoral guidance independently identifies an opportunity to correct apparent non-participation before sanction. No actual 2026 non-voting enforcement, final election result, individual remediation completion, new Bellbrook-specific failure or coordinated misconduct established.
