## 2. Analysis — Accepted evidence
### A01 — Audited NSW accounts distinguish deficit improvement from rising actual debt
ACCEPTED — incremental primary documentary evidence; 27 November 2026 publication; reporting year ended 30 June 2026. Extends SR-516, which admitted the ministerial statement but had not examined the underlying accounts.
Treasury's original 239-page Report on State Finances was retrieved. Selected PDF page images 5, 9, 21, 22, 26 and 177 (one-based) examined, including independent audit opinion, Table 2.1 and Note 33.
FACT within the accounts: GGS net operating deficit was $2,852 million in 2025–26, against $5,029 million in the reclassified 2024–25 comparatives. Original 2025–26 budget deficit $3,427 million; favourable variance $575 million. The improvement against the June 2026 revised estimate was $197.3 million. Total State Sector deficit was $5,251 million against $8,685 million in the comparative year.
COUNTER-EVIDENCE: GGS net debt rose from $105.2 billion to $114.4 billion; gross debt rose from $166.0 billion to $177.6 billion. TSS net debt rose from $161.3 billion to $177.6 billion. GGS interest expenses increased $531.9 million. The release compares debt with forecasts and quotes the Treasurer describing debt as down. The audited year-to-year balances show increases, not decreases. This is a baseline distinction, not a finding of deliberate deception. The deficit improvement is genuine within the accounting measure. The independent auditor's opinion supports fair presentation of the consolidated accounts, not every political interpretation.
Treasury's explanation includes higher grants, investment distributions relative to budget, and taxation receipts; higher employee and other costs offset some improvements. Comparative amounts were reclassified; the 2025 Auditor-General report had reported a $5.1 billion deficit for 2024–25. Preserve both publications; do not treat the revised comparative as an error without examining the accounting reconciliation. The ministerial release itself acknowledges emerging revenue and borrowing-cost pressures.
UNKNOWN: independent verification of historical superlative, exact earlier projection comparisons, future fiscal results, causal attribution of the whole improvement to policy, distribution of service effects and actual household benefit. No full financial forensic examination undertaken.
INFERENCE: annual operating balance, debt stock, forecast variance, and public-service outcomes are different measures. Positive fiscal performance on one measure need not imply improvement on all others.

### A02 — Historical Black Friday enforcement follow-through recovered
ACCEPTED RETROSPECTIVELY — ACCC release 112/26, 29 September 2026; conduct concerns 2025 promotions. Extends SR-533 A02 historical monitoring evidence.
FACT as regulator-reported action: James Lane paid $19,800 following one infringement notice, and Vuly paid $39,600 following two notices concerning allegedly misleading representations of sale duration. ACCC reports James Lane had planned an extension before the advertised deadline, and Vuly reset countdown timers while discounts continued or increased. These are regulator allegations or reported investigative findings, not judicial determinations. Both businesses committed to compliant future advertising, according to ACCC.
COUNTER-EVIDENCE: monitoring progressed to reported payments; corrective commitments were obtained. Genuine deadlines are not inherently unlawful. Payment of a notice is not treated as adjudicated liability. The businesses' independent responses and actual implementation were not examined.
UNKNOWN: individual consumer losses, restitution, effectiveness of future controls, 2026 sweep outcomes. No earlier match for these named actions found in targeted full-report screening; historical duplication remains possible.
INFERENCE: detection, infringement action, payment, restoration and prevention are separate evidentiary stages. This closes part of the enforcement-outcome gap identified in SR-533 for the historical sweep, not the separate 2026 sweep.
