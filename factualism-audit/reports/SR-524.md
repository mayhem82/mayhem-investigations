## 2. Analysis — Accepted evidence

### A01 — NDIS registration revocations and effective-date integrity
ACCEPTED — primary regulator enforcement register, last updated 27 November 2026. The register records registration revocations effective 27 November for Majestic Living Australia Pty Ltd (Dubbo NSW) and Circles of Life Disability Support Solutions Pty Ltd. The stated basis is the delegate's reasonable belief concerning compliance with applicable NDIS Practice Standards, informed by audit outcomes. This establishes the regulatory decisions and stated basis, not independent proof of each underlying contravention. A separate corporate entry (Rabiola Pty Ltd) describes a decision made 9 November, taking effect 18 December 2026 at 5 pm ACST, despite the listing's current-status heading describing the action as in force. Preserve the stated effective date rather than assuming immediate operation. Counter-evidence: the register provides reasons, scope and dates; this is an identifiable protective intervention. UNKNOWN: underlying audits, review or stay status, actual participant transitions, continuity of supports and outcomes. No claim of individual harm or broader sector prevalence.
Source: https://www.ndiscommission.gov.au/compliance-and-enforcement-actions
Mechanism (inference): regulatory removal and safe continuity of participant support require separate verification. A register status label cannot replace the operative date.

## 3. Findings — Longitudinal structural analysis

M01 — Enforcement and continuity. SR-516 identified historical remediation as distinct from procedural correction; A01 adds the distinct requirement to examine continuity of support following regulatory intervention. A status label with a future operative date also requires temporal verification before being relied on by the public.

M02 — Digital and physical alternatives. A02 and A05 identify separately documented maintenance arrangements. Compare SR-521's court digital interruption and registry closure evidence. Existence of an alternative channel is not proof of equivalent accessibility. The systems are unrelated; no common failure or actual exclusion established.

M03 — Public record visibility. A04 and A07 extend SR-523's timing distinction into FOI access. Access for a requester, publication to the general public, availability of actual files and long-term discoverability are separate conditions. No statutory breach or intentional concealment has been established.

M04 — Interim protection and local capability. A03 and A06 provide constructive examples of reusing capacity and supporting regional skills. Compare SR-521 disaster housing transition: temporary support must be followed through to human outcomes rather than assumed effective or permanent. Training is not a substitute for accessible professional services.
