### A01 — Electoral participation figures must retain their processing status
ACCEPTED — primary VEC statistics page updated 28 November 2026, daily rows 17–27 November. Summing the 11 published rows yields 2,282,370 early votes and 339,039 postal votes returned by mail. The latter are received envelopes/returns as defined by VEC, not necessarily formal votes or votes admitted to the count; the VEC explicitly warns scrutiny has not occurred. The figures are calculated totals from the displayed daily rows, not separate published totals. The first day listed is 17 November, before ordinary early voting commenced 18 November; no attribution of all first-day votes to any specific accessibility channel. The count plan schedules counting from 6 pm on 28 November and return of writs by 19 December; mailed postal ballots must arrive by 4 December, subject to applicable requirements. Positive evidence: publication of daily channel-specific totals, explicit warning about scrutiny and staged count plan. UNKNOWN: final turnout, formal and rejected postal votes, duplicates across participation channels, district-level outcomes, storm-related access effects and final election results. Spreadsheet not analysed. Do not subtract the returns from the earlier 750,000 expected postal participants (SR-534) to infer lost votes; estimate, returns and final admitted votes are different quantities. Structural mechanism (inference): measurement-stage substitution can obscure the difference between participation attempts, receipt, scrutiny and counted votes.
