Council Tracker Machinery

This is the operational map of the current tracker/ system. It is not the legacy CouncilWatch dashboard and it is not the public translation layer.

Source of truth: Kempsey Shire Council remains the public-record authority. Council Tracker preserves provenance and derived/audit state needed to interrogate those records; it does not replace Council as the datastore.

Non-negotiable machinery boundaries

No subject-keyword matter seeding.
No automated finding or verification promotion.
Council language is not outcome proof.
Absence from the observed corpus is not disappearance or failure.
Mechanical integrity is not evidentiary verification.
Public release remains deny-by-default and human-authorised.

Operational interface generated from the current Council Tracker machinery architecture. No claim is made here that a live pipeline run has executed or that any current Council outcome is verified.
