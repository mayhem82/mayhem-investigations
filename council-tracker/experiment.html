Experimental Protocol

Literal specification of the council audit experiment. No analogy is used to define the protocol.

BUILD STAGE

The audit machinery remains under construction. A mechanism being described or coded does not establish that it works.

Experimental subject

A deliberately selected council and the records it makes available constitute the real-world audit environment. The fact that the subject is a council is known and intentional. The experiment does not pretend otherwise.

System under observation

The developing AI-assisted audit machinery: its mechanisms, behaviour, constraints, failures, corrections, retained state and reproducible capabilities.

Primary question

What can the developing audit machinery independently identify, preserve, structure, connect, analyse and substantiate from the selected council’s records when it is not supplied with a preselected list of matters, actors, issues, patterns, allegations, conclusions or investigative targets telling it what to find?

Development question

As failures are exposed and corrections are introduced, can those corrections become persistent governed mechanisms that survive later use, reduce repeated human intervention and increase the depth of audit the system can sustain without sacrificing evidence discipline?

Experimental control

The experimenter may know the council and deliberately select the source environment. What is withheld from the machinery is a prewritten audit answer. It is not seeded with a selected wrongdoing narrative, target actor, predetermined matter list, expected finding or conclusion to prove.

Roles

Experimenter

Chooses the subject and constraints, observes operation, challenges failures, supplies corrections and determines subsequent tests.

AI/software apparatus

Performs defined operations within the audit system. It is a tool and experimental apparatus, not a co-investigator or co-owner of the experiment.

Council record

Provides the continuing real-world material on which the machinery operates.

Audit machinery

The developing system whose capabilities and limitations are being tested longitudinally.

Capabilities under test

Discovery

Recognition of potentially significant material without a preselected matter list.

Connection

Recurrence, relationships, chronology, continuation and change across separately published records.

Audit depth

Progressive examination of a signal without every subsequent path being manually prescribed.

Evidence discipline

Preservation of provenance and separation of source, observation, derived structure, inference, verification and finding.

Verification discipline

Resistance to promoting similarity, repetition or internal agreement into proof.

Governance

Increasing capability without bypassing constraints, uncertainty or release controls.

Transfer

Retention of useful mechanisms across different matters without encoding the earlier matter as a universal rule.

Continuity

Incorporation of later council records while maintaining coherent audit state.

Public translation

Production of understandable public output without severing its evidentiary lineage.

Reduced repeated correction

Whether previously exposed failures become mechanisms rather than recurring human repair tasks.

Procedure

select council → acquire records → preserve source and provenance → extract → observe → detect candidate signals and relationships → assemble evidence → deepen audit paths where warranted → test claims and verification boundaries → retain state and later records → control public release → record behaviour, failure, correction and change

Pre-contact baseline and first contact

Before substantive council records enter the machinery, construction and mechanical testing establish the pre-contact state. First substantive contact with the selected council is a distinct experimental transition. The machinery at that point provides the baseline against which later demonstrated development can be compared.

After first contact, council records exert real audit pressure. Failures and limitations exposed by ambiguity, recurrence, contradiction, chronology, governance structure, missing material, later records and downstream accountability work are retained as experimental evidence.

Developmental pressure cycle

council contact → audit pressure → limitation or failure exposed → correction → mechanism created or changed → retest → demonstrated capability retained → new council material or deeper audit pressure

The intended transformation is of the machinery, not of the evidentiary record. A successful correction should become a governed mechanism capable of surviving later use and different matters rather than a council-specific patch. Repeated need for the same human correction counts against a claim of development.

Full accountability pathway under test

open audit → evidence assembly → verification → findings → accountability analysis → Advocacy Package → Enforcement Notice → remedies → remedy verification → continuing audit

The Advocacy Package tests evidence-linked external translation. The Enforcement Notice tests conversion of established findings into specific corrective demands while preserving the distinction between established authority, advocacy and recommendation. Remedy construction tests whether the machinery can identify corrective pathways, responsible capacity, implementation evidence and measures of success. Remedy verification tests actual implementation and effect rather than accepting promises, announcements or expenditure as closure.

Failure at any downstream stage returns to the developmental pressure cycle. Downstream outputs are therefore not merely products of the experiment; they are additional tests of the machinery.

Literal mature-state proposition

The experiment tests whether repeated council contact, failure, correction, retesting and retention can produce machinery that sustains increasingly complex, previously unspecified audit-to-accountability-to-remedy work under accumulated governance with progressively less repeated corrective intervention from the experimenter. This state is not presumed and cannot be established by design intent or isolated success.

External governance transformation

The furthest developmental proposition is not simply that the audit machinery becomes more capable. It is whether governance architecture demonstrated through the machinery can ultimately become governance architecture within other systems. Evidence would require an external system to acquire and sustain relevant provenance, evidence discipline, verification boundaries, accountability controls, correction mechanisms and recursive governance within its own operation rather than remaining dependent on continuous enforcement by the originating audit machinery.

A corrected outcome, institutional influence, adoption of a recommendation or deployment of another copy of the audit machinery is not by itself evidence of this transformation.

Integrated governed architecture

The complete pathway is part of the system under observation, not merely a sequence of outputs: source acquisition and preservation → provenance → audit discovery → evidence discipline → chronology and relationships → analysis → verification → findings → accountability attribution → Advocacy Package → Enforcement Notice → remedy construction → implementation evidence → remedy verification → recursive audit → correction → retained governance mechanism.

Cross-cutting controls include contradiction handling, explicit unknowns, persistent state, continuity, case isolation, human authority, release control, regression detection, transfer and recursive correction. Failure in any pathway stage or cross-cutting control is experimental evidence about the maturity of the governed architecture.

Experimental variables

Observed variables include the kinds of audit signals produced; accuracy of source lineage; quality of relationships and chronology; false connections; unsupported promotion of claims; depth reached without manual path prescription; frequency and type of human correction; whether corrections persist; transfer of mechanisms to different matters; continuity across later records; whether public outputs remain traceable and bounded; performance through Advocacy Package, Enforcement Notice, remedy construction and remedy verification; and whether demonstrated governance mechanisms can eventually persist within an external system.

What counts as evidence of development?

Observable and reproducible operation. A capability must survive use, remain governed and produce auditable outputs. A successful isolated answer is not enough. Code existing is not enough. A human repeatedly repairing the same failure is evidence that the relevant capability has not yet become reliable machinery.

Failure treatment

Failure is retained as experimental evidence rather than hidden. The record should identify what failed, the conditions under which it failed, what correction was introduced, what mechanism changed, and whether later operation demonstrates that the change persisted.

Longitudinal character

This is not a single-run benchmark. Council records continue to appear and the machinery continues to change. Later material therefore tests both audit capability and whether earlier development survives contact with new conditions.

Publication boundary

Internal audit signals and candidate relationships are not automatically public findings. Public output must remain downstream of evidence lineage, verification state and release controls. The experiment also tests whether useful public accountability information can be produced without collapsing those distinctions.

Current experimental state

Neutral machinery components have been constructed through source boundary, preservation and provenance, discovery, extraction, source-bound observation, candidate relationships, recurrence, evidence assembly, verification boundary and deny-by-default release control. Mechanical test code exists for multiple components. The complete system has not yet been executed and validated end to end, so those components are construction progress rather than a demonstrated complete audit capability.

Next gate

Construct and execute an end-to-end mechanical audit of the machinery itself using controlled non-substantive test material. Record the actual results and failures. Only demonstrated behaviour may be carried forward as established capability before substantive council auditing begins.

The Philosopher’s Stone is documented separately as an analogy for interpreting development. Its terminology does not define this protocol, its variables, evidence states, procedure or endpoint.
