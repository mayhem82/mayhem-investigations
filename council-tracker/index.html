The Experiment

Development and testing of investigative machinery against a selected council

BUILD STAGE

The Experiment is still under construction. Machinery described here is developmental and must not be treated as a completed, validated or operational experimental system.

A council has been deliberately selected as the experimental subject. The machinery is not given preselected matters, actors, issues, patterns, conclusions or investigative targets telling it what to find.

BUILD STAGE

The investigative machinery is still under construction. Capabilities are recognised only when they have been demonstrated.

Experimental subject: a deliberately selected council and records obtained from that council.
Selected council records
↓ Preserve source material and provenance
↓ Identify and structure observations
↓ Detect candidate relationships and recurrence
↓ Assemble evidence with source lineage
↓ Test verification boundaries
↓ Apply controlled release rules

The experiment observes what the machinery can independently identify, preserve, structure, connect, analyse and substantiate, and whether demonstrated capabilities become reproducible mechanisms rather than requiring the same human correction repeatedly.
